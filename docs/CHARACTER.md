# Mr Bubs — Character Authority

## Canonical rule

The original full-body Mr Bubs artwork is the immutable visual authority. Do not overwrite it. New poses, sprite sheets, expressions, animations, and optimized exports are derived assets.

## Identity anchors

- warm dark-brown skin
- vivid lavender swept-back hair
- full dark charcoal beard and moustache
- oversized bronze/brass steampunk goggles with dark reflective lenses and mechanical detailing
- cream patterned shirt with dark tie
- compact, stylized mascot proportions

Keep these anchors recognizable across scale, pose, expression, and rendering mode.

## Modes

### Mini Bubs
Head-and-upper-torso presentation intended for small interactive UI. Directional states communicate pointer tracking; expression states communicate reactions.

### Full Bubs
Complete character presentation retaining the full outfit and silhouette. Intended for larger illustrations and future idle, walk, wave, point, celebrate, think, sit, and peek animations.

## Asset policy

- Preserve the canonical master unchanged.
- Put generated or edited derivatives in the appropriate Mini/Full directories.
- Keep directional frames aligned consistently.
- Prefer transparent backgrounds for production assets.
- Version material visual changes rather than silently replacing assets used by existing projects.
